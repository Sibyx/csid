Two scopes, because a capture is a mixture on two axes. An ambient channel interleaves several PHY types at once, and an illuminated one interleaves a metronomic injector with whoever else is talking. The spectrum, phase, impulse-response, Doppler and metronome views are scoped to one class and one transmitter — mixing geometries changes the array widths under the plots, and mixing transmitters produces timing statistics that belong to none of them. The waterfall can still show every class, placed by frequency. Nothing is dropped from the capture: csiscope only reads the live stream, and every record still lands in capture.raw.

The second chain cancels CFO/SFO by conjugate multiplication before the Doppler transform. Without it the spectrum smears.

Centre frequency sets wavelength, not walking speed. Inference cannot resolve 6 GHz, whose channel numbers overlap 2.4 GHz.
