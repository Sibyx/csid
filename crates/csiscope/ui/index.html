An ambient channel interleaves several PHY types at once. The spectrum, phase, impulse-response and Doppler views are scoped to one class — mixing geometries would change the array widths under the plots and make a time series compare unlike things. The waterfall can show all of them, placed by frequency. Nothing is dropped from the capture either way: csiscope only reads the live stream, and every record still lands in capture.raw.

The second chain cancels CFO/SFO by conjugate multiplication before the Doppler transform. Without it the spectrum smears.

Pin the frequency to make the Doppler speed axis exact. Inference cannot resolve 6 GHz, whose channel numbers overlap 2.4 GHz.
